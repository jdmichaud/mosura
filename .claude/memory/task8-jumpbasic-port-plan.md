## THE FAITHFUL MECHANISM (grounded)
Ghidra `JumpBasic::analyzeGuards` (jumptable.cc:1046) starts with `CircleRange rng(toswitchval)` (bool {0|1}) at the CBRANCH condition, then does maxpullback(=2) iterations of `vn = rng.pullBack(readOp, &markup, usenzmask)` — **CircleRange::pullBack transforms BOTH the range AND the varnode backward through each defining op**. So `INT_LESS(INT_ADD(index,-1),8)` pulls back: {1} → (through INT_LESS,8) range [0,8) on `index-1` → (through INT_ADD,-1) range [1,9) on `index`. A GuardRecord is pushed at EACH step (vn+range). `calcRange` (:1120) then, for a pathMeld candidate vn, finds a matching GuardRecord via `valueMatch`(:637)/`oneOffMatch`(:684)/`quasiCopy`(:719) and intersects its CircleRange. `findSmallestNormal`(:1165) walks the PathMeld picking the smallest-range vn = normalized switch var. `buildAddresses` emulates each value. **pullBack is the range-transforming step mosura's normalize lacks — THAT is the whole fix.**
